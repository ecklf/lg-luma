Add movable fixed-size Home widgets on a 4×4 placement grid. Greeting combines weekday/date and a time-based greeting in two columns and one row, restoring the original typography. Weather spans one column and two rows. By default Greeting sits on row one and Weather spans rows two/three. Preview placements with the remote or pointer, reject overlaps and out-of-bounds positions, and save positions without changing the app dock. Date visibility/format live within Greeting settings; there is no separate Date widget or configurable name.
