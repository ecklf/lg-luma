Add movable fixed-size Home widgets on a 4×4 placement grid: weekday/date and a larger time-based Greeting each span two columns and one row; Weather spans one column and two rows. The default arrangement places Date first, Greeting second, and Weather across the third/fourth rows. Preview placements with the remote or pointer, reject overlaps and out-of-bounds positions, and save positions without changing the app dock. Greeting has no configurable name; legacy names are ignored.
