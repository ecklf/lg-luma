Home becomes a grid of widgets you arrange yourself: hold OK on a block to move, resize or remove it, and add widgets back from "+ Add widget". Six themes (General › Home theme) set a layout, wallpaper and look in one step. New widgets show crypto prices (Binance) and this week's season premieres (TVmaze), which open in the app you choose. Every widget follows one design standard (docs/design.md): weather and crypto prices look alike in every theme, and the favourites, inputs and premieres are rows with the same header and a carousel of cards.
