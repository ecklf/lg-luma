A widget store: Add widget → Widget store… lists data-only widgets from a signed catalog, shows what each connects to and where its key goes, and installs it onto Home. Store widgets draw with Luma's own row cards and dialog, refresh off the SDL thread, run their jq transforms in a limited child process, keep keys in a private file and take them from the remote or a phone by QR code. The first widget in the catalog is esports: live and upcoming matches of eight games, with live scores on your own free PandaScore key.
