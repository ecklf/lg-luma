Add web access: pair a phone or computer once in **Settings → System → Phone & computer**, then change Luma from its browser without the remote. The page looks like Luma, shows a live picture of the TV, and covers General, Apps, Background (including your own photos), Widgets and System. A remote under the picture controls Luma, opens the TV's inputs and listens to the keyboard; with **Control the whole TV** it reaches every app and adds volume, screen off, a sleep timer and power off. Web access is off by default, works only on the home network, and paired devices can be removed on the TV at any time.
