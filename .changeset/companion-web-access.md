Add web access: pair a phone or computer once in **Settings → System → Phone & computer**, then change Luma from its browser without the remote. The page looks like Luma, shows a live picture of the TV, and covers General, Apps, Background (including your own photos), Widgets and System. Web access is off by default, works only on the home network, and paired devices can be removed on the TV at any time.
