A native OpenGL launcher for LG webOS TVs.

  • Liquid Glass surfaces, a floating favorites dock, and an animated app drawer. In General → App drawer, choose tvOS (default) for a peek of the next app row or Minimal for a compact, lower dock without the peek. Enable General → Show app names to display names beneath selected drawer cards. Off by default for compact spacing; favorites stay unlabeled.
  • Add, configure, move and remove Greeting, Weather, Crypto and Stocks from a 6×4 Home grid. Add multiple Weather, Crypto and Stocks widgets with independent cities and symbols. Greeting combines the weekday/date and greeting in four columns and one row; Weather offers 2×2 and compact 1×1 sizes. Sizes stay fixed, and widgets cannot overlap.
  • Launch apps directly. Manage favorites, hidden apps, and uninstall actions in Settings → Apps. Left/right navigation wraps between the first and last app in favorites and the app drawer.
  • Sort apps A–Z or by local launch counts in Settings → General, keeping favorites in your chosen order.
  • Start with the bundled Midnight background or upload your own. Toggle gradient shading and enable automatic rotation between available backgrounds.
  • Select inputs directly from compact pills with icons and connection dots. Customize names and visibility. USB storage reported by webOS appears alongside HDMI; selecting it opens an installed LG media browser. USB discovery and media opening require verification on your TV firmware.
  • Choose an icon for each input from bundled SVGs, including HDMI, USB, Apple TV, computers, consoles, PlayStation, NVIDIA Shield, Steam, streaming devices, Nintendo Switch, Nintendo Switch 2, and Xbox. Choose Automatic to restore label-based icon detection.
  • Choose short Back on Home, long Back, or Off for Recent apps; browse, launch, or close individual apps.
  • Add webhook shortcuts for automations.
  • Weather with selectable Breezy Weather, Lucide, Kickstand or Kuankuan day/night icons, temperature, and an optional location name. Open Luma or TV settings from the clock.
  • Preview Standard, No icon, and Compact weather widget styles using your selected icons and location-name visibility.
  • Read offline credits and license references in System → Attributions, with remote or mouse-wheel scrolling.
  • Optional soft UI feedback sounds in Settings → General, off by default.
  • Navigate with SDL-recognized game controllers: D-pad or left stick, OK, Back and Options. Controller availability depends on TV firmware and the SDL backend.
  • Optional 1×1 crypto and stock price widgets with configurable symbols, key-free feeds, relative quote ages, and daily percentage changes with colored arrows. Stock quotes may be delayed.
  • Check for Luma updates and install verified releases from Settings → System. Upgrades to v4 require confirmation of the Home Chooser migration steps before downloading.
  • Select Luma as your full-screen Home app with Home Chooser, without Luma's takeover scripts. Home Chooser manages Home-key routing and restoring LG Home; behavior depends on TV firmware.

Available in Catalan, Czech, Dutch, English, French, German, Hungarian, Italian, Polish, Portuguese (Brazil), Russian, Simplified / Traditional Chinese, Spanish, Swedish and Vietnamese.
