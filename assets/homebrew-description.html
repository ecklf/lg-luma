A native OpenGL launcher for LG webOS TVs.

  • Launch apps and inputs, organize favorites, and browse your app library.
  • Upload custom backgrounds and enable automatic background rotation.
  • Customize input names and visibility.
  • Add webhook shortcuts for automations.
  • Personalize with greeting, clock, and weather.
  • Check for Luma updates and install verified releases from Settings → System.

Available in Dutch, English, French, German, Italian, Polish, Portuguese (Brazil), Russian, Simplified / Traditional Chinese, Spanish and Vietnamese.
