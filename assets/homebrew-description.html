A native launcher for rooted LG webOS TVs, inspired by Apple TV.

Launch apps and inputs, organize your favorites, and browse your app library with remote or Magic Remote pointer controls. Choose or upload custom backgrounds, enable automatic background rotation, and personalize input names and visibility. Add webhook shortcuts and configure them on your phone or computer by scanning a QR code. Customize the greeting, clock and app-name display, or show weather for a city of your choice.

Available in Dutch, English, French, German, Italian, Polish, Portuguese (Brazil), Russian, Simplified / Traditional Chinese, Spanish and Vietnamese.

Requires a rooted TV and elevated Homebrew Channel.
