A native OpenGL launcher for LG webOS TVs.

  • Liquid Glass surfaces, a floating favorites dock, and an animated app drawer.
  • Add, configure, move and remove Greeting and Weather from a 4×4 Home grid. Greeting combines the weekday/date and greeting in two columns and one row; Weather occupies one column and two rows. Sizes stay fixed, and widgets cannot overlap.
  • Launch apps directly. Manage favorites, hidden apps, and uninstall actions in Settings → Apps.
  • Sort apps A–Z or by local launch counts in Settings → General, keeping favorites in your chosen order.
  • Upload custom backgrounds, download photo packs on demand, toggle gradient shading, and enable automatic background rotation.
  • Select inputs directly from compact pills with icons and connection dots. Customize names and visibility.
  • Choose an icon for each input from bundled SVGs, including HDMI, USB, Apple TV, computers, consoles, PlayStation, NVIDIA Shield, Steam, streaming devices, Nintendo Switch, Nintendo Switch 2, and Xbox. Choose Automatic to restore label-based icon detection.
  • Hold Back to open Recent apps; browse, launch, or close individual apps.
  • Add webhook shortcuts for automations.
  • Weather with colored day/night condition icons, temperature, and location. Open Luma or TV settings from the clock.
  • Preview Standard, No icon, and Compact weather widget styles in Settings.
  • Optional soft UI feedback sounds in Settings → General, off by default.
  • Check for Luma updates and install verified releases from Settings → System.

Available in Catalan, Czech, Dutch, English, French, German, Italian, Polish, Portuguese (Brazil), Russian, Simplified / Traditional Chinese, Spanish, Swedish and Vietnamese.
