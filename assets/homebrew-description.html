A native OpenGL launcher for LG webOS TVs.

  • Liquid Glass surfaces, a floating favorites dock, and an animated app drawer.
  • Launch apps directly. Manage favorites, hidden apps, and uninstall actions in Settings → Apps.
  • Upload custom backgrounds, toggle gradient shading, and enable automatic background rotation.
  • Select inputs from a compact dropdown with icons and connection dots. Customize names and visibility.
  • Choose an icon for each input from bundled SVGs, including HDMI, USB, Apple TV, computers, consoles, PlayStation, NVIDIA Shield, streaming devices, Nintendo Switch, Nintendo Switch 2, and Xbox. Choose Automatic to restore label-based icon detection.
  • Add webhook shortcuts for automations.
  • Weather with colored day/night condition icons, temperature, and location. Open Luma or TV settings from the clock.
  • Check for Luma updates and install verified releases from Settings → System.

Available in Catalan, Czech, Dutch, English, French, German, Italian, Polish, Portuguese (Brazil), Russian, Simplified / Traditional Chinese, Spanish, Swedish and Vietnamese.
