A native launcher for rooted LG webOS TVs, inspired by Apple TV.

Launch apps and inputs, organize your favorites, and browse your app library with remote or Magic Remote pointer controls. Customize backgrounds and input labels, and add weather for a city of your choice. Available in Dutch, English, French, German, Italian, Polish, Portuguese (Brazil), Russian, Simplified Chinese, Spanish and Vietnamese.

Requires a rooted TV and elevated Homebrew Channel.
